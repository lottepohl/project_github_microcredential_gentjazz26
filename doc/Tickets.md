# Ticket information

## Availability
Tickets can be bought online via https://tickets.gentjazz.com/ or directly at the venue (Bijlokesite).
There is a maximum nr of 5000 tickets available per day.

## Price
Prices range from 60 to 180eur, depending on the *chosen seating place* (but *NOT* on the day).
Tickets for GentJazz2026 are bought **for each day independently**, unlike in previous years when you could purchase 2-day bundles.
